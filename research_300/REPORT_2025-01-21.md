## A finding beyond this day: most downloads used prod-0, not the preferred release

Every earlier download took prod-0. The preferred-release files name another feed on many of those days: in
December 2025 two of nine (14 and 17 December, mlatonly-0); in January 2025 three of eight (8, 9 and 16, staging-0); in
July 2025 four of nine (9, 11, 13 and 16, staging-0); and on the scored days **10 of 31 in January 2026 and 14 of 31 in
July 2026** (staging-0). No day's point count was anomalous, so prod-0 was not grossly incomplete there, but the
preferred feed may see more aircraft. Re-fetching those days from their preferred release is a label-free step that
could raise coverage; nothing was re-run here.
